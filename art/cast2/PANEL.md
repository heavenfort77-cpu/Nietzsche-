# Panel: night-1 cast and the insecurity constructs (22 Nov 2026, round 1)

Roster: the 22 Nov panel roster is in the Project docs (`claude/Panel-review-22-Nov.md`), which this chat could not read. Rebuilt from the judges the GDD records (rounds 1 and 3): Nietzsche and Dazai through their work, core gamer, casual phone player, jam judge, Camus and Chaplin through their work, professional auguste clown, clinical psychologist, indie critic, streamer, mobile producer. Scores are my judgement of the renders (`lineup_front.png`, `lineup_playsize.png`, `sheet_crew.png`, `sheet_constructs.png`), not a playtest. Out of 5.

| Judge | Crew | Constructs | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nietzsche | 3 | 4 | The Stare is the abyss looking back (the crowd's heads, one eye). Shame wearing his bowler makes the mask his own. |
| Dazai | 4 | 4 | The child in his father's cap, eyes to the side, straight mouth: the one who sees, right. The Whisper should be visibly the child grown long, or the link is lost. |
| Core gamer | 3 | 4 | Four constructs, four silhouettes, four answers. The rigger's cable is 1.6 px wide at 64 px: too thin to read as a jump. |
| Casual phone player | 4 | 4 | Each figure's verb shows in its pose (horn held out, pie held out, cable in hand). The Light's lens-eye says "it watches you". |
| Jam judge | 4 | 4 | Nine new figures in the approved style, distinct, not recolours. Crew read as a circus only by the strongman. |
| Camus | 3 | 3 | Crew are people at work; good, the clown is not the only one labouring. Constructs are all fear, none show the absurd revolt. |
| Chaplin | 4 | 3 | The crew give him company (my round 3 note). Shame hiding behind gloves is close to sentimental; keep its motion small. |
| Auguste clown | 3 | 4 | Props girl with a cream pie and the strongman's bulb horn are real ring props. The stagehand's rope coil reads as a horn at play size. |
| Clinical psychologist | 4 | 4 | Shame (hiding the face) and the Stare (being judged) are accurate shame imagery; nothing reads as self-harm. The Light as exposure is right. |
| Indie critic | 3 | 4 | Constructs are built from the stage (spotlight, audience, the clown's own hat), which answers "two games stapled together". Crew are plain. |
| Streamer | 3 | 5 | The Stare is the clip: the mound turns and one eye opens. |
| Mobile producer | 4 | 4 | 4k-9.5k triangles per web GLB, 1.7 MB for nine: inside the phone character budget (10-15k). |
