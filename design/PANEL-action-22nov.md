If the rule is adopted the panel says the game loses: the inversion where every press feeds the enemy (the one mechanic unlike other jam entries), and the paint-flaking stillness image with the Dazai reading that doing is the mask. It gains: something to do in every scene (the stranger's "nothing to do", "felt like hiding"), and one verb from ring to mirror to final, so the shop and the gags matter.

What it would build first: the Light with an active answer (turn, face it, gag into the lamp), stillness a short breath and then a drain; a Performer prototype where polished gags are copied back, off-cue or hurt gags land, and each one used flakes paint. Risk for a jam judge: "his own" against "bought" is unreadable without a clear tell and scores as random.

## Red team

- P1, Camus (voted yes): "The stranger's complaint was 'nothing to do' and 'felt like hiding', which is a feedback and clarity failure, not proof that stillness is wrong. A clown's sickness is that he cannot stop acting. If gags always cure him, the thing the crowd pays for becomes the medicine and the game says 'perform harder'." Story writer changed to no.
- P2, Indie critic: "A double beaten by hits is a boss fight like any other. 'He is himself' then means nothing in play, and the shop's cost (every act teaches the Performer) is cancelled by simply out-hitting it." No change.
- P6, Story writer (voted yes): "If he wins a clean fight by performing, 'I quit' has no cause and reads as a random menu. It holds only if the fight costs him his acts or his paint." Core gamer changed to no.
- P7, Jam judge (voted yes): "A game where the right inputs win is fair, and a fair game is not out to get you. The theme then rests only on the crowd laughing at real hurt and the shop arming the double, and both must be shown." No change.
