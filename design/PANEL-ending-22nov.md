Where the panel differs from the owner's wording: 9 seats want no new buttons (the old controls are the choice), 3 want worded buttons; 9 keep the Performer beaten by standing still after the constructs are fought; 5 refuse to show performing again as a loop; 4 reject the seen crowd and 4 reject the bow.

What it would change first: offer the choice through the old controls with a clear tell (act buttons light again, the exit is lit); ending 2 returns to night 1's curtain with the mask back on. Risk for a jam judge: he presses an old button out of habit, lands on night 1, reads it as a restart or a bug, and never learns a quit ending existed.

## Red team

- E1, Jam judge (voted yes): "A judge plays once and sees one ending, so half will see the weaker one. Two endings double the work on the part of the game nobody can reach yet, while night 1 scores 2/2/2 with the stranger. A choice in the last minute does not fix 'I watch the clown fall with nothing to do'. And offering 'perform again' makes quitting optional, which softens 'life is out to get you'." Mobile producer changed to no.
