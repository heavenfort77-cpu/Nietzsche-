# Panel: a small win at the end of the Mirror World, and the performance after the cue (22 Nov, session 15)

Owner's pitch (22 Nov 12:52): "theme and gameplay not to be lost and end of mirror world must be a small win like life, it may be depressing but small win even through the theme. else the whole game is too depressing." Owner also asked (item 9) where the performance after the cue is: the GDD has the act in the centre ring with the camera low behind the back rows (night loop step 4) and the crowd reacting "with bodies and sound, not text" (step 7); the night-1 prototype shows a text card instead.

Method: the same 12 judges as `PANEL-night1-gameplay.md`, in rounds of proposal, counter-proposal and vote until 7 of 12 agree. Judgement of design and code, not a playtest. Night 1 today, from the code: Mirror World ends (win keeps all shards, loss half) -> fog -> "In vain was all our labour." alone -> shards card -> shop at the mirror -> end card. Nothing at the end is his own.

## Question A: the small win

### Round 1: proposals

| # | Proposal | From |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | One real laugh: as he leaves the mirror, one unseen person offstage laughs for real, once. Plants the final night's unseen clapping. | Chaplin, streamer |
| A2 | He wipes one stripe of paint off himself: after the Nietzsche line, a prompt; the player's press wipes one paint layer (Mask -1). The only way paint comes off without the shop. | Clinical psychologist, auguste clown |
| A3 | A gift from the child: back at the mirror a paper nose or a drawing is left on the table. | Dazai |
| A4 | He gets up by himself: after the line, he rises from the chair under his own power, no one calls him. | Camus |
| A5 | A shard that is his: winning keeps one shard the shop cannot take, shown apart on the ledge; it counts on the final night. | Core gamer, mobile producer |
| A6 | Warmer room: the cube room's gloom eases a step after a win. | Indie critic |

Nietzsche (through his work), jam judge: no proposal of their own in round 1; for "anything that is done by him, not given to him".

### Round 2: counter-proposals

- Indie critic against A3: a gift is a reward from outside; the line just said all labour is vain, so the answer must be his own act. Dazai agreed, and withdrew A3: "Yozo's one who sees does not give, she only sees." The child keeps the GDD role (watches him leave on the last night).
- Core gamer against A2 as free: a Mask -1 on every win undercuts the shop's cost. Psychologist's answer: one layer, never below the paint he started the night with; the shop and the act add more than one layer a night, so the face still thickens over the run, only slower. Core gamer accepted with that floor.
- Camus against A2 on a loss: a loss should not wipe paint, or losing costs nothing. But a loss must still end on something (the owner's "like life"). A4 for a loss: he stands up by himself. Psychologist agreed: getting up after a bad night is the most honest small win there is.
- Mobile producer against A5: a second shard type is a new system for one beat; A2 already shows on his face in every scene, which is a tangible result. Core gamer withdrew A5 if the wipe shows on the end card.
- Nietzsche against A6: the room should not change for him; he changes. A6 withdrawn.
- Streamer: A1 alone is a sound effect; joined to A2 it is the clip: he wipes the stripe, someone laughs once, warmly, and stops. Chaplin: the laugh comes after the wipe, never before, so it answers him, not the act.
- Jam judge: the line "In vain was all our labour." then a small act that is not vain: the contrast is the theme, not a softening of it. Nietzsche: that is amor fati: the labour is vain and he does one more small thing anyway.

### Round 3: vote on the merged proposal

**Merged A (A2 + A4 + A1):** after the Nietzsche line, back at the mirror (the same seat he entered from):
- **Win:** prompt "Wipe away one line of paint". The player's press wipes one paint layer off his face (Mask -1, never below the paint he started the night with); one real laugh offstage answers it, once.
- **Loss:** prompt "Stand up". The player's press makes him rise from the chair by himself; no laugh, no meter change.
- Either way the beat is one press, under 3 s, and the end card names it ("He wiped away one line." / "He got up.").

| Judge | Vote | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Nietzsche | yes | Vain labour, one more small act anyway. |
| Dazai | yes | His own hand on his own mask, once. |
| Core gamer | yes | With the floor; it is a reason to win, not just survive. |
| Casual phone player | yes | One tap, something nice. |
| Jam judge | yes | Contrast keeps the theme. |
| Camus | yes | The loss branch is the one I care about. |
| Chaplin | yes | Laugh after the wipe. |
| Auguste clown | yes | Taking off the face is the end of every show, so this rehearses it. |
| Clinical psychologist | yes | Agency, small and real; the loss branch models recovery without reward. |
| Indie critic | yes | His act, not a gift. |
| Streamer | yes | The clip. |
| Mobile producer | no | Wants the wipe automatic on a win (no extra press). Outvoted: the press is the point (his act). |

**Consensus: 11 of 12, merged A.** All values in CONFIG behind a switch (`smallWin`), with the end card line.

## Question B: the performance after the cue (item 9)

### Round 1: proposals

| # | Proposal | From |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | After the cue, cut to the zoom-out ring from the GDD's low seat behind the back rows (clown about 64 px): the act replayed as its gags, one per chain, Perfect = snap and confetti, miss = the real fall backwards with the big laugh (agreed item 1), crowd bodies react by result. The text card shrinks to a caption with the Laughs. | Jam judge, Chaplin, streamer |
| B2 | Play the cue itself in front of the ring (zoom in) instead of the cube room, then B1. | Auguste clown, indie critic |
| B3 | Zoom-out walk-on before the cue: he steps into the spotlight, the house goes quiet, then the zoom in. | Dazai, Camus |
| B4 | Keep the text card, add crowd sounds only. | Mobile producer |

### Round 2: counter-proposals

- Jam judge against B4: the GDD says bodies and sound, not text; fix 2 needs the fall to be seen getting the laugh. Mobile producer withdrew B4, asked that B1 be skippable after its first second (tap) on replays.
- Core gamer against B2 now: the cue is accepted (owner: "cue is ok") and its background is a separate page; moving it is a second job. B2 kept as a later item, not in this batch.
- Psychologist for B3: the walk-on is where he puts the face on for them; the crowd going quiet is the pressure. Mobile producer: under 3 s. Merged into B1 as its opening.
- Streamer: the replay must show the house reacting to his pain more than to his skill: the biggest row movement on the fall.

### Round 3: vote on the merged proposal

**Merged B (B3 + B1):** zoom-out ring from the low seat behind the back rows, clown fitted to about 64 px: walk-on into the spotlight (about 2 s, house quiets) -> the cue (unchanged, zoom in) -> back to the zoom-out: the act replayed as gags in the order played (about 1 s each), the crowd silhouettes react per beat (Perfect: rows rock; miss: the fall backwards and the biggest roll of laughter, item 1), a standing house only on an ovation -> a one-line caption with the Laughs (the fix 2 headline) -> walk back. Tap skips after the first second. Box-and-shape ring for the prototype (rule 1); the approved ring set later.

Votes: yes 10 (all but mobile producer and core gamer). Core gamer: wants B2 too (the cue in the ring); mobile producer: wants the skip on the first play as well. **Consensus: 10 of 12, merged B.** B2 stays on the list for later.
