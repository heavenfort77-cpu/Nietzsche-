# Stranger test kit (one real person, about 15 minutes)

Who: someone who has never seen the game, heard the idea or read the design. Not a game developer if you can help it. Phone or laptop, whichever they have.

What to send (share the artifact from claude.ai first; only you can share it): the night-1 link, "The Clown and the Mirror: night 1 (prototype)", https://claude.ai/artifact/9TVEmYRb4nwTnKv5Wpnbjp

What to say, and nothing more: "It's a short game prototype. Play it until it ends or until you'd normally stop. Think out loud if you can."

Don't explain anything, don't answer questions while they play ("do what you think"), don't say it's about Dazai or Nietzsche or that it's sad.

## Watch for (write the time next to each)

1. The first moment they hesitate or ask what to do, and on which screen.
2. Where they would have stopped if you weren't there.
3. Any laugh or smile, and at what.
4. Whether they read the quote cards or press past them.
5. In the act: do they miss on purpose after the first time, or try to play clean?
6. In the Mirror World: do they find out that standing still beats the Light without help?
7. In the shop: do they notice the reflection stands up and comes closer?

## Ask after (their words, not yours)

1. What is this game about?
2. Who is the clown, and what does he want?
3. Was anything funny? Was anything sad?
4. What did the crowd laugh at most?
5. What would make you play night 2?
6. One thing you'd change.
