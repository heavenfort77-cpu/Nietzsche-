The Clown and the Mirror: the mask cue

A painted mask closes in on his face. Press when it fits the thin outline. The drum plays on every fit.

Desktop: Space = hit, K or Shift = take. Phone: right button = hit, left button = take.

Night 1: hit only. Night 2 and later: the whiteface steps in (his face closes in instead of the mask): press take. On the last beat of each chain, take instead of hit turns the grin upside down.
